How to Avoid Money Scams: 12 Red Flags Before You Take a Side Gig

The 12 biggest red flags of side-hustle, payment and 'make money fast' scams — and what to do if you've been targeted.

SCBy the Snap.Cash Editorial Team · Updated Sep 30, 2026 · 6 min read

Scammers target people who need money fast. Learn these red flags and you'll dodge 95% of them.

12 red flags

  1. You must pay to start, for "training" or a "starter kit".
  2. They send a check and ask you to send part of it back.
  3. Payment only via gift cards, crypto or wire transfer.
  4. Guaranteed income or "no work required" claims.
  5. Job offers without an interview, via text or social DMs.
  6. Pressure to act immediately.
  7. Requests for your bank login or one-time passcodes.
  8. "Cash flipping" — send $100, get $1,000 back.
  9. Recruit-your-friends income with no real product.
  10. Poorly written emails from free email accounts claiming to be big companies.
  11. Reshipping packages from home.
  12. Overpaying you for an item and asking for a refund of the difference.

If you've been targeted

  • Stop contact and don't send more money.
  • Contact your bank or payment app immediately.
  • Report it to your national consumer protection agency (e.g. the FTC in the U.S., the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre, Action Fraud in the UK).
