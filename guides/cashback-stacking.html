Cashback Stacking: How to Earn 2–3× Rewards on Every Purchase

Step-by-step guide to stacking cashback portals, coupons, rewards cards and receipt apps on the same purchase.

SCBy the Snap.Cash Editorial Team · Updated Sep 30, 2026 · 6 min read

Stacking means earning rewards from several sources on the same purchase. Done right, 2% becomes 6–10% on everyday spending.

The four layers

  1. Cashback portal — click through a shopping portal before buying online.
  2. Store coupon or promo code — check the portal allows codes, or cashback may be voided.
  3. Rewards credit card — only if you pay the full balance monthly; interest wipes out rewards instantly.
  4. Receipt app — scan the receipt for extra points.

In-store stacking

Link a card to card-linked offers, use grocery rebate apps, then scan the receipt. Gas apps can add cents per gallon on top.

Rules that protect your cashback

  • Disable ad blockers when clicking through a portal (tracking needs cookies).
  • Don't open another site between clicking and checkout.
  • Screenshot your order confirmation in case you need to file a missing-cashback claim.
