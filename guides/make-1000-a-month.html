How to Make an Extra $1,000 a Month (Realistic Plans by Skill Level)

Three realistic plans to earn an extra $1,000 per month on the side, with the hours, pay rates and math for each.

SCBy the Snap.Cash Editorial Team · Updated Sep 30, 2026 · 8 min read

$1,000 a month extra is about $230 a week. At $20/hour net, that's roughly 11–12 hours a week. The key is picking work that pays enough per hour that the goal fits your schedule.

Plan A: No special skills (12–15 hrs/week)

  • Delivery or rideshare on weekends: ~8 hrs × $18 net = $144/week
  • Pet sitting or task gigs: ~4 hrs × $22 = $88/week
  • Total ≈ $1,000/month

Plan B: Computer skills (8–10 hrs/week)

  • Freelance writing, design, VA work or AI data training at $25–$35/hr.
  • 9 hrs × $28 = $252/week ≈ $1,090/month

Plan C: Assets (2–4 hrs/week)

  • Rent a spare room or parking space.
  • Peer-to-peer car sharing on days you don't drive.
  • Can exceed $1,000/month with little weekly time — but check local rules, insurance and your lease.

Don't forget taxes

Set aside 20–30% of side income for taxes. See side hustle taxes explained.
