High-Yield Savings Accounts: How to Earn More on Cash You Already Have

What high-yield savings accounts are, how much more they can earn, what to check before opening one, and how compound interest works.

SCBy the Snap.Cash Editorial Team · Updated Sep 30, 2026 · 6 min read

If your emergency fund sits in a traditional savings account paying near 0%, you're leaving free money on the table. High-yield savings accounts (HYSAs), usually from online banks, often pay many times the national average rate.

How much difference does it make?

On a $10,000 balance, the difference between 0.5% and 4% APY is about $350 a year — for doing nothing but moving your money. Rates change often; always check the current APY.

What to check before opening

  • Deposit insurance: FDIC (U.S.), CDIC (Canada), FSCS (UK) or your country's equivalent.
  • Minimums & fees: the best accounts have no monthly fees and low or no minimums.
  • Promotional vs. standard rate: some high rates are temporary.
  • Transfer speed: how fast you can move money back to checking.
  • Sign-up bonuses: some banks pay a cash bonus for new deposits — read the conditions.
