# Snap.Cash — Concept & Revenue Strategy

## 1. The decision: what Snap.Cash should be

**Concept:** *Snap.Cash — Fast, legit ways to make, save & find cash.* It's a "quick money" hub: side-hustle directory, cashback/rewards comparison, bonus alerts, free money calculators, guides, a video hub, giveaways, and a **2-minute Cash Finder quiz** that works as the lead-generation engine.

**Why this idea beats the alternatives.** Each option is scored on three things: (1) AdSense CPC, (2) whether search demand already matches the domain's meaning, and (3) whether it's legally clean.

| Option | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| P2P payment app / wallet | ❌ Reject | Needs money-transmitter licensing, has massive capex, and sits right next to the discontinued Snapcash feature and Cash App (trademark risk). |
| Payday / instant-loan lead site | ⚠️ Avoid as the core | Highest CPC, but AdSense and Google Ads restrict it, and state regulators watch it closely. Keep it as an *optional* "compare borrowing options" branch only. |
| Generic finance blog | ➖ Weak | The space is crowded, and the name adds nothing to it. |
| **Quick-money hub + lead funnel** | ✅ **Pick** | The name *means* "fast cash." That matches high-volume, evergreen queries like "make money fast," "make $100 today," "cash back apps" and "side hustles." It sits in the **personal-finance vertical, one of the highest-CPC AdSense verticals**. It also stacks five revenue lines on the same traffic. |

## 2. Revenue stack (in order of expected $/visitor)
1. **Lead generation.** The Cash Finder quiz captures name, email and optional phone with consent. You can monetise leads by CPL/CPA with partners (fintech, gig apps, insurers, education), sell newsletter sponsorships, and own the audience.
2. **Affiliate / referral offers.** Cashback apps, bank bonuses, gig platforms, budgeting apps and tax software.
3. **Google AdSense.** Every page has pre-placed responsive slots (header, in-content, sidebar). Ads never sit inside forms or calculators.
4. **Sponsorships.** Sponsored placements, giveaway prize sponsors and newsletter slots.
5. **Reader support.** One-time donations, a monthly "Insider" membership, and a pledge form.
6. **Domain / JV inquiries.** A top bar on every page links to web.works/contact.

**Rough model at 50k monthly visits** (assumptions, not a promise): AdSense at a $8–20 page RPM × 1.6 pages/visit ≈ **$640–1,600**. A 3% quiz opt-in gives 1,500 leads × $1–5 blended ≈ **$1,500–7,500**. Affiliate clicks at 2% × $3–10 EPC ≈ **$1,000–3,000**. Lead gen is the biggest line, so the quiz sits above the fold on the homepage and has its own dedicated page.

## 3. Research — 39 sites analysed (plus 4 inner pages)
NerdWallet (+calculators, careers), Bankrate (+mortgage calculator), The Penny Hoarder (+side-gigs), Side Hustle Nation, Forbes Advisor, Money Crashers, Credit Karma, YNAB, Rakuten, Ibotta, TopCashback, Fetch, Survey Junkie, Prolific, Upwork, Fiverr, Doctor of Credit, Financial Samurai, Making Sense of Cents, Ramsey Solutions, Smart Passive Income, Clark.com, LendingTree, Credible, Experian, MoneySavingExpert, GoFundMe, Patreon, Buy Me a Coffee, Gleam, Sweepstakes Fanatics, DoSomething.org, Wise Bread, Money Under 30, The Simple Dollar, GOBankingRates, Calculator.net, DollarSprout, Freecash.

**Patterns adopted:**
- **Multi-step quiz, one question per screen, contact details asked last** (LendingTree/Credible). Separate unchecked SMS and partner-consent boxes (TCPA/CASL).
- **Trust microcopy** ("No credit check", "Never sells your data", "Unsubscribe anytime") placed right beside the form (Bankrate/Credible).
- **Calculators with live results and an action at the end** (Bankrate/NerdWallet). Calculator.net shows that tool pages alone can carry display ads.
- **Filterable directory** by speed, tools and pay (Sweepstakes Fanatics / DollarSprout).
- **Verb-based navigation**, a green "money" palette, a trust-stat strip, advertiser disclosure at the top, and a "How we make money" page (Money Crashers, Forbes Advisor, Side Hustle Nation).
- **Newsletter repeated** on each page plus a desktop-only exit-intent offer, shown once per session (GOBankingRates).
- **Buy Me a Coffee-style preset amounts, Patreon-style tiers, and "where your money goes" transparency.**
- **Gleam-style giveaways:** referral bonus entries, full Official Rules, NO PURCHASE NECESSARY, and a Canadian skill-testing question.
- **Honesty guardrail:** no fake ratings, user counts or "$X paid out" claims. Every stat on the site is true on day one.

## 4. Trademark position
The brand is always written **Snap.Cash**, with an original lightning-coin logo and green palette. It never uses "Snapcash" (one word), Snapchat's ghost/yellow, or Cash App's "$" green-square trade dress. It offers no P2P payment functionality. A full non-affiliation and copyright notice is in `disclaimer.html`, and a short version is in every footer. **Recommendation:** get a trademark clearance search before spending on paid ads or registering the mark.
