How to Make $100 Today: 15 Legit Ways That Actually Pay Fast

15 legit ways to make $100 today — from selling items and gift cards to delivery gigs with instant pay-out. No scams, no upfront fees.

SCBy the Snap.Cash Editorial Team · Updated Sep 30, 2026 · 7 min read

Need $100 by tonight? The fastest legit money almost always comes from one of three places: things you already own, gig apps that pay out instantly, or one-off local tasks. Here's how to pick the right one in minutes.

In this guide
  1. Sell what you already own
  2. Gig apps with instant pay
  3. Local one-off tasks
  4. Online quick wins
  5. What to avoid

1. Sell what you already own (fastest for most people)

  • Electronics: old phones, tablets, game consoles and headphones sell within hours on local marketplaces. Price 10–15% below comparable listings to sell same-day.
  • Unused gift cards: gift card exchange marketplaces typically pay 70–92% of face value.
  • Furniture & baby gear: high local demand; offer "must go today" pricing.
  • Brand-name clothing & sneakers: fashion resale apps and local consignment.
Pro tip: Great photos in daylight + an exact model number in the title = faster sales and higher prices.

2. Gig apps with instant or same-day pay-out

Food and grocery delivery apps usually let you cash out your earnings the same day for a small fee. Onboarding can take a few days, so this is fastest if you're already approved. Typical pay: $15–$25/hour before car costs — use our gig driver profit calculator to see your real number.

3. Local one-off tasks

  • Help a neighbour move, assemble furniture or clear a yard ($25–$60/hr).
  • Pet sitting or dog walking via pet-care apps.
  • Babysitting for friends or local parent groups.
  • Car washing and detailing in your neighbourhood.

4. Online quick wins

  • Paid research studies: academic studies pay far better than surveys ($8–$20/hr).
  • Website testing: $10+ per 20-minute test, but tests go fast.
  • Cashback on something you already need: not "income," but real money back.

5. What to avoid

  • Anything that asks you to pay a fee to start a "job".
  • Deposit-a-check-and-send-money-back requests (classic fake check scam).
  • "Cash flipping" or doubling money schemes on social media.
